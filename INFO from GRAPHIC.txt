The Million-Dollar Question
Amidst the ongoing controversy and discussion, a pivotal question emerges for us as Christians: Can we reclaim Halloween for the Lord? It's a question that ignites spirited debate. Some argue that everything belongs to the Lord, referencing Psalm 24:1 as their foundation. They believe this means we shouldn't let Satan claim Halloween because it was never his to begin with. However, this perspective raises important considerations.

An In-depth Look at Scripture
Let's examine Psalm 24:1 more closely. This verse indeed highlights the Lord's ownership, emphasizing that the earth and all its fullness belong to Him. But is this a universal claim that extends to all days and celebrations, like Halloween? To gain a clearer understanding, we should look at the broader context of the verse, which emphasizes God's ownership over creation.

Defining "Everything"
Does "everything" truly mean everything when we apply it to specific days and events? For instance, is the Fourth of July the Lord's day? What about the celebration of Satanist High Holy Day or Mardi Gras? The idea that "everything" is the Lord's raises complex questions about what falls under this umbrella.

The Real Day of the Lord
To find a clear answer, we should turn to Scripture. Isaiah 56:2 provides insight into the true day of the Lord. It emphasizes keeping the Sabbath holy, refraining from evil, and aligning with the Lord's covenant. This biblical passage doesn't mention Halloween as a holy day. Instead, it underscores the sanctity of the Sabbath, a day specifically designated as holy.

A Profound Revelation
In conclusion, the day of the Lord, as described in Isaiah 56, is the Sabbath—a day of rest and holiness. Nowhere in Scripture is Halloween identified as a holy day. While the desire to reclaim Halloween for God is admirable, it's essential to approach this issue with a deep understanding of scriptural context and intention. We must remember that God's design for holiness is clearly outlined in His Word, and we can find our path by seeking His guidance and wisdom.
